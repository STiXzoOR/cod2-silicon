| `R_MarkFragments`, `0xf47ca` | Finite-input bounds, 4096-surface collection limit, material `stateBits[1] & 0x30`, triangle winding and squared facing threshold, six-plane clipping, interpolation and fragment output agree. Nonfinite classification remains unresolved below. |

`R_MarkFragments` expands four input corners by both signs of
`radius * axis[0]`. Its four edge planes use normalized cross products; the
remaining planes are the positive and negative projection axis offset by the
radius. Triangle rejection uses negative facing or
`dot * dot < 0.25f * normalLengthSquared`. It preserves points in the
half-unit plane band, rejects polygons with no front point, copies polygons
with no back point, and linearly interpolates position, two lightmap
coordinates and three normal components on crossings.

The final output preserves the clipped normal without renormalization;
texture coordinates use `0.5f / radius`, then add `0.5f`. Binormal is
`cross(normal, axis[1])`, tangent is `cross(binormal, normal)`. Fragment
material is the requested mark material, with the surface lightmap index and
running first-point index. The exact fragment-count limit and the
`returnedPoints > maxPoints - 3` early return are unchanged. Renderer output
color is deliberately untouched; the cgame caller supplies it.

Mac scalar constants were read from mapped binary addresses: `0x326190` is
`0.5f`, `0x3261a0` is zero, `0x3261bc` is `0.25f`, and `0x3261f4` is `-0.5f`.
The original uses scalar single-precision multiply/add/divide in these paths.
Native pointer-sized surfaces, children and fragment materials remain typed;
the source accesses the same semantic fields as the original offsets.

## Remaining edge and considered changes

At `0xf5124`/`0xf512b`, an unordered plane distance follows the non-front
branch. At `0xf50d2`–`0xf50d8`, `ucomiss` followed by `setbe` classifies NaN as
on-plane (side 2). Native `d >= -0.5f` instead selects behind (side 1).
The instruction-level difference is confirmed; a reachable gameplay input
and resulting fragment difference have not been established. The ordinary
zero-length edge is not sufficient: `Vec3Normalize` leaves a zero vector
unchanged. This edge remains open rather than being reported equal.
